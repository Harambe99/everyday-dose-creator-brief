05 · Compliance

Say this. Never say this.

This is the section that gets videos taken down. Read it twice.

✓ Say this

  • "Half the caffeine of a normal cup" or "45 milligrams"
  • "No jitters", "calm energy", "steady focus"as your own experience
  • "Supports balanced cortisol" and "less stress"the brand's own words
  • "Coffee raises cortisol, and the afternoon cup still does"
  • "L-theanine has been studied for calm and focus"
  • What really happened to you after you drank it

✕ Never say this

  • "Lowers cortisol" or "clinically proven to lower cortisol"
  • Anything about weight loss, belly fat, or "cortisol belly"
  • "Cures", "treats" or "fixes" anxiety, sleep, gut issues or any condition
  • "80% less caffeine"it's about half
  • A discount, "selling out" or "last chance"unless the brand gave it to you that day
  • Results you didn't have
  • "My doctor told me to take it"unless that's true
  • "Cancel any time", "easy to cancel" or "no risk"lots of reviews complain about cancelling, so stay off it
  • That it fixes acid reflux or any stomach conditionquote a review instead, word for word
06 · Craft

Do this. Don't do this.

✓ Do

  • Drink it on camera. Scoop, froth, first sip.
  • Show the product for 5 to 10 seconds.
  • Talk like you talk. Simple words, short sentences, like you're telling a friend.
  • Shoot it on your phone, in your real life. Kitchen, car, desk. Natural light.
  • Keep it one take. Small mistakes are fine. Real beats polished.
  • Say the CTA exactly as given.

✕ Don't

  • Don't open with a question"Do you have high cortisol?"
  • Don't open with a list intro"3 signs..."
  • Don't make skits that need a second person.
  • Don't use studio lights, heavy edits or stock footage.
  • Don't bash coffee or coffee drinkers. We're upgrading the cup.
  • Don't name other brands or coffee shops.
  • Don't read the ingredient list like a label. Pick one and say what it does for you.
  • Don't show a body or face before and after.
